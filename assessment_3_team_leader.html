Section 01 — Agency Foundation

Is Your Business Built Around You — or Built to Run Without You?

Most agencies are built around the founder's personality and relationships. That works until it doesn't. This section surfaces how dependent your agency is on you personally — and where structural gaps exist that will limit growth.

💡 Answer for how the agency actually operates today, not how you intend it to work. The gap between intention and reality is where the real diagnostic value lives.
Section 02 — Agent Onboarding & Culture

What Happens in the First 90 Days When a New Agent Joins?

The first 90 days determine whether a new agent becomes productive or becomes a churn statistic. Most agencies lose agents not because the opportunity is bad — but because there was no structure to help them succeed during the most critical window.

⚠️ If your current onboarding process is "here's your login and good luck," you already know what this section will tell you. The goal is to identify the specific gaps and their cost.
Section 03 — Financial Infrastructure

Does Your Agency Have the Financial Clarity to Make Confident Decisions?

Agency-level financials are more complex than solo agent finances — and the stakes are higher. Commission disbursement, agent-level P&L, split structures, and overhead allocation all require real systems, not spreadsheets you update when you have time.

📈 The most common financial gap in growing agencies: knowing the team GCI but not knowing whether the agency itself is profitable after all costs are accounted for.
Section 04 — Technology at Scale

Can Your Tech Stack Support Multiple Agents — or Is It Straining Under the Weight?

Technology that works for one agent often breaks down at 5–10 agents. The compliance workflow, CRM management, transaction visibility, and communication tools that work fine solo become bottlenecks the moment you add agents who need to depend on them.

💻 At the agency level, the question is not just "do we have the tools" — it's "do agents actually use them consistently, and does leadership have visibility into what's happening across all transactions?"
Section 05 — Agent Development & Retention

Are You Building Agents — or Just Housing Them?

There is a meaningful difference between an agency where agents close deals and an agency where agents grow their businesses. Agents who feel developed and supported stay. Agents who feel they could get the same experience anywhere leave — usually to a competitor who sells them on a slightly better split.

🌟 Retention is not a culture initiative — it's an operational one. The agents who stay long-term are almost always the ones who feel they're becoming better at their craft, not just getting access to a brokerage brand.
Section 06 — Growth Opportunities & Additional Revenue

Are You Capturing the Full Revenue Potential of Your Agency?

Most agencies leave significant revenue on the table — not from lack of closings but from uncaptured adjacencies: in-house ancillary services, referral revenue, property management, commercial, and the social media and marketing infrastructure that would reduce agent marketing spend.

🔥 This section is about opportunity, not obligation. Not every agency should pursue every option — but every leader should know which ones they're deliberately declining and which ones they haven't considered yet.
Section 07 — Staff, Roles & Operational Infrastructure

Do You Have the Right People in the Right Roles — or Are You Still Doing Everything?

The transition from solo agent to agency leader almost always stalls at the same point: the leader is still doing agent work, admin work, and management work simultaneously. These are three completely different jobs. Understanding which roles your agency actually needs — and in what order — is the inflection point most leaders miss.

👥 The most expensive staffing mistake at the agency level: hiring another agent when what you need is an operator. More production volume without more management infrastructure makes the chaos worse, not better.
Section 08 — Revenue, Profitability & Growth Targets

Do You Know Whether Your Agency Is Actually Profitable?

GCI is not profit. Many agency owners are generating impressive transaction volume while keeping less per deal than their agents. This section evaluates whether your financial model is sustainable — and what levers actually move agency profitability.

💰 This section requires knowing your numbers. If you don't have them in front of you, estimate where you can and flag what you need to go calculate. Identifying the gaps is the point.
