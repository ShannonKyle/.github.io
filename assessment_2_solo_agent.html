Section 01 — Lead Generation

Where Does Your Business Come From — and Is It Reliable?

Most agents know they need more leads but haven't diagnosed why the pipeline is inconsistent. These questions reveal whether the problem is volume, conversion, follow-up speed, or database neglect.

💡 Answer based on what is actually happening right now, not what you intend or plan. Honest gaps are the most valuable data this assessment surfaces.
Section 02 — Pipeline & Transaction Operations

From Contract to Close: Where Does Your Process Actually Break Down?

Operational friction costs agents deals they've already earned. This section maps your real process and identifies exactly where time is lost after you have a client.

⚠️ Think about an actual transaction from the last 30 days as you answer. Real failure modes — not your ideal process — are what matter here.
Section 03 — Administration, Bookkeeping & Compliance

Task Audit: What Are You Doing That Someone Else Should?

Admin work is necessary but almost never the highest use of an agent's time. This section finds what to delegate first — and whether the fix is a hire, a tool, or a workflow change.

📋 Rate tasks on how much pain they cause you — not how important they are. High-pain, low-complexity tasks are prime outsourcing candidates regardless of how small they seem.

Section 04 — Technology & Software

Are Your Tools Working For You — or Just Costing You Money?

Technology should reduce friction and multiply your time. But there's a critical distinction most agents miss: a compliance tool (stores documents, confirms filing) vs. a business management tool (tells you proactively what needs attention today). You likely need both — but may only have one.

💻 Audit what you have before considering what to add. Most agents find savings and capability gaps in the same 30-minute review.
Section 05 — Finances & Overhead

Do You Know Your Numbers Well Enough to Make Good Decisions?

Most solo agents run on gut feel. Without knowing your monthly overhead, net per deal, and break-even transaction count, you cannot confidently decide when to hire, what to spend, or whether your current brokerage arrangement makes sense.

📈 Not knowing these numbers yet is extremely common. The goal here is to surface what you should know and don't — so you know exactly what to go calculate this week.
Section 06 — Hiring & Delegation Readiness

What Kind of Help Do You Actually Need?

Hiring before you're ready is expensive and often fails. Not hiring when you're ready caps your income. This section determines where you are on that spectrum — and whether the right answer is a VA, TC, buyer's agent, or something else entirely.

👥 The most common mistake: hiring a buyer's agent when what's actually needed is an admin. These are completely different problems with completely different solutions.
